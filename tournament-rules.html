Rules

01

No hacking, no exceptions

Any confirmed or suspected use of hacks, aimbots, wallhacks, or any third-party tool results in immediate disqualification of the entire squad. No warning, no refund.

02

Mobile devices only

Emulators and PC/keyboard-mouse setups are not allowed. Players found using an emulator will be disqualified along with their squad.

03

Squad registration is final

Once a squad is registered with 4 player IDs, no substitutions are allowed after the room ID is shared.

04

Room ID and password

Shared in the Telegram group 10 minutes before match start. Do not share the room details outside the group.

05

No teaming

Squads found cooperating with or sparing another squad on purpose will be disqualified.

06

Connection issues

Re-entry into a running match is not possible. Disconnects during a match are not the organizer's responsibility — no re-match will be arranged for individual disconnects.

07

Scoring

Points are based on placement plus kills. Final standings are calculated after all scheduled matches, and the organizer's decision on scoring is final.

08

Respect the organizers and other players

Abusive behavior toward organizers or other squads in the group or in-game voice chat can lead to disqualification.
